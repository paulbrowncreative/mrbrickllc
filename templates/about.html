A family masonry business

Mr. Brick LLC is a family-owned and operated masonry contractor based in Eastpointe. For more than 25 years we've repaired and built brick, block, stone and concrete for homes and businesses across Southeastern Michigan — chimneys, porches and steps, driveways and walks, patios, foundations and retaining walls.

Our motto is simple: honest, practical and quick. Honest means telling you what your masonry actually needs, including when a smaller repair will do. Practical means building for Michigan weather — footings below frost depth, mortar matched to your brick, crowns and caps that keep water out. Quick means fast, free quotes and clear communication from first call to clean-up.
