Well-built pavers are one of the most durable surfaces you can put down in Michigan. The individual units can move slightly with frost without cracking, and any area that does settle can be lifted and relaid. When pavers sink, rock, spread or sprout weeds, though, it's almost always the base or the edges, not the pavers themselves.

## Why pavers sink or dip

- **Thin or uncompacted base.** A proper paver base is excavated deep enough for the traffic it will carry and filled with compacted aggregate in layers. Pavers laid on a little sand over soil will settle.
- **Water washing out the base.** Downspouts and poor grading carry base material away.
- **Soft spots** that weren't dug out before the base went in
- **Too much bedding sand**, which compresses unevenly over time

## Why pavers heave or rock

- **Frost** in a base that holds water
- **Tree roots** pushing up from below
- **Missing joint sand**, which lets pavers move against each other

## Why paver edges spread

Pavers need **edge restraint**, a buried edge or a concrete or masonry border that holds the field in place. Without it, the outer rows creep outward and gaps open up across the surface.

## Why weeds and ants show up

Weeds grow where joint sand has washed out and wind-blown soil has taken its place. Keeping joints full of sand, especially polymeric joint sand where appropriate, and sweeping regularly are the best prevention.

## How sunken pavers are fixed

1. **Lift the pavers** in the affected area and set them aside
2. **Correct the base.** Remove soft material, add aggregate and compact in layers.
3. **Relay the bedding layer** to the right thickness
4. **Reset the same pavers** in the original pattern
5. **Restore edge restraint** if it's missing or failed
6. **Refill and compact joint sand**
7. **Fix the water.** Redirect downspouts and correct grading.

Because the same pavers go back down, the repair usually blends right in.

## Simple paver maintenance

- Sweep regularly and keep joints full of sand
- Pull weeds while they're small
- Keep downspouts discharging away from paved areas
- Optional sealing to enhance color and help hold joint sand
